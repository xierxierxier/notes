# C-C-Clownfish (fan translation)
**Original Online Title: 小丑丑丑鱼 (Xiǎochǒu chǒu chǒu yú/C-C-Clownfish)**
**Original Printed Title: 七秒 (Qī miǎo/Seven Seconds)**

## **Author: 巫哲 (Wū zhé)**

**Unofficial Fan Translation: xiermifen**
I claim no copyright of the story or characters or anything like that, only my own translation :)

## Synopsis:

Boy meets Merman.

Shen Dong is one of two lighthouse keepers on a small, otherwise uninhabited island off the coast. Sky, sea, birds, every day is the same until someone new turns up.

Cao Mu claims to be a clownfish, and has the memory capacity to match, but there's no way that could be true, right? Because that would make him a merman...

Is Cao Mu telling the truth and what does he want? Can he even remember?

And why exactly is Shen Dong on this island anyway?

All this and more cute shenanigans in Wuzhe's *C-C-Clownfish*~
