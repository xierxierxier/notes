# Chapter 13 - Shen Dong, The Pure Hearted
[[clownfish - 0]] | [[clownfish - 12]] | [[clownfish - 14]]

Shen Dong sat at the table with a notebook and pen and the very newest train timetable. Leaving the island wasn't difficult. Getting onto land and setting about to search for the mermaid Yu Xiaojia was also not too difficult.

The difficult thing was figuring out how to safely get Cao Mu there.

From where they were in the south up to the northern city they would have to cross a vast swath of the map. Cao Mu didn't have an ID card and they couldn't always stay on a train.

The amount of time Cao Mu could spend as a human was limited and not even he knew how long it was. All he knew was that after some amount of time he'd start to feel uncomfortable and have to dive into the water to transform back.

Shen Dong estimated the amount of time this was and tried to figure out what trains they could take.

"You're writing," Cao Mu said excitedly as he watched.

"Oh." Shen Dong continued to mull over the issue. Cao Mu could spend a whole night sleeping as a human then have to go into the water at dawn, yet his limit was quite short during the day. Did it have something to do with temperature then?

He continued to think this over as he doodled a line on the paper.

"What word's that?" Cao Mu asked, pointing at the marks.

"They're not words," replied Shen Dong. So, if they took a train at night, then Cao Mu could probably maintain his human form for the whole journey, but at dawn he had to get into the water. Would a bathtub do?

"Write something and let me see." Cao Mu used a finger to poke his pen.

"When you turn into a fish, then back to a human, h-how long is the cooldown time?" Shen Dong wrote the word 'pig' on the paper.

"Dunno, just as long as I'm in the water." Cao Mu took the pen from him and earnestly started to imitate what he'd written on a corner of the paper.

Earnest was the right way to describe it. Shen Dong was a little speechless. From Cao Mu's point of view, the word 'pig' was upside down and so, when he copied it out, it was also upside down.

"T-this way." Shen Dong rotated the page around for him. As long as he had water, then a bathtub would be okay.

"What's the word?"

"Cao Mu."

"... You're lying," Cao Mu used the pen to dot the paper twice. "Cao Mu is two words, and I can read them, and I can read Guanhua Town Centre Primary School Grade 3 Class 4 too, and Yu Xiaojia, and..."

"Pig." Shen Dong picked up the train timetable. The distance from the ship's dock to the main city wasn't too far, just a normal bus ride away. Then finding a hotel with a bathtub would probably be okay too, and then...

"I'm not a pig, why you scolding me?"

"I mean... this word is p-pig." Shen Dong chuckled.

"Oh." Cao Mu picked up the pen, intent on copying the word again, but after one stroke, he stopped. "You're still scolding me!"

It took Shen Dong a while to recover from his laughing and sit up straight again. "If I take the ship, can you follow in the water?"

"No problem. I often follow after it." Cao Mu's reply was calm but his eyes had irrepressible excitement in them. "Are we really going this afternoon?"
